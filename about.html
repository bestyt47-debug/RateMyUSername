Our mission

Creators, gamers and everyday scrollers share one problem: the name you pick today follows you into every video, server and comment section tomorrow. Our mission is simple. We help you secure a memorable, brandable online identity before you build an audience around a name you'll outgrow.

How it works

Rate My Username pairs a fast, secure database tool with data-driven platform naming guides. Drop in a handle and get an instant score, compare two names head-to-head, or roast one for fun. Rating never needs an account. Sign up only if you want to save results and climb the leaderboard.

Our blog goes platform by platform, from TikTok and Discord to Instagram, YouTube and Twitch, with 100 fresh ideas, real character limits and name-change rules for each. Every suggestion actually fits where you'll post.

Free, always

No paywalls and no gatekeeping, just a clean tool for the internet's naming problems. Got feedback or an idea? Find us on Instagram at @officialratemyusername.
