In Fortnite, your name is the first thing players see in the lobby and the last thing they see in the elimination screen. It shows up in kill feeds, Party Chat, spectate mode and clips that end up on TikTok. A sharp name tells teammates what to expect. A goofy one lowers the tension. And a memorable one turns a random duo into a friend request.

Your in-game name is your Epic Games display name, so it also appears across other Epic titles. It has to be 3 to 16 characters, which is short enough to force you to be creative. Below are 100 names within that limit across five styles, from try-hard builders to cozy zero-build wanderers. Pick one, tweak it and test it in your Epic account settings.

How to pick a Fortnite name that fits

Sixteen characters goes fast, so a few habits keep your name readable in the kill feed.

  • Aim for 12 characters or fewer. Long names get cut off in lobbies and on the elimination screen.
  • Skip lookalike characters. Swapping I and l or 0 and O makes your name hard to read and hard for friends to find.
  • Match your playstyle. A sweaty name for a chill player or vice versa sets the wrong expectations.
  • Keep it clean and clip-friendly. Your name may end up in viral clips and streams. Avoid anything you wouldn't want on a highlight reel.
  • Remember it's your Epic name. It appears across Epic games, so pick something you'd be happy to see outside Fortnite too.

FAQ

How long can a Fortnite name be?
Your display name, set through your Epic Games account, can be 3 to 16 characters. It can include letters, numbers and some symbols, but a clean mix of letters and numbers is safest.
How often can I change my Fortnite name?
You can change your Epic display name once every two weeks for free in your Epic Games account settings. That's frequent enough to test a new name, but not so often you should switch on a whim.
Is my Fortnite name the same as my Epic name?
Yes. Your Fortnite display name is your Epic Games display name, so it appears in other Epic titles too. On some consoles the platform gamertag may show in certain menus.
Will I lose my skins or progress if I change my name?
No. Your cosmetics, stats and progress are tied to your account, not your display name. Only the name shown in lobbies and kill feeds changes.
Can I use special characters or emojis?
Epic allows some symbols but not emojis, and unusual characters can look broken on other platforms. Stick to letters, numbers and simple punctuation.
