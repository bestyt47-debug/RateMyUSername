Pinterest works more like a search engine than a social network. People aren't scrolling to see friends. They're searching for outfit ideas, dinner recipes and living room makeovers, and they follow whoever keeps showing up with the best pins. That changes what a username needs to do. It should tell a stranger what you pin about before they've even opened your profile.

A clear, niche-friendly handle also builds trust when someone lands on one of your pins from search. They glance at your name, see 'weeknight dinners' or 'small space ideas', and decide you're worth a follow. Pinterest usernames are 3 to 30 characters using letters, numbers and underscores. Below are 100 ideas across five popular niches. Grab one, add your own twist and check availability in your profile settings.

How to pick a Pinterest username that gets found

Because Pinterest leans on search, a few keyword-friendly habits help.

  • Include your niche. Words like home, recipes, fits or DIY help both people and search understand your profile.
  • Use underscores for readability. Pinterest usernames allow letters, numbers and underscores, so separate words to keep them clean.
  • Keep it under 25 characters. Short handles are easier to share and look better in profile URLs.
  • Match your other socials. A consistent name across Pinterest, Instagram and your blog builds recognition.
  • Think of the profile name too. Your display name can be longer and more keyword-rich, so let the username stay simple.

FAQ

How long can a Pinterest username be?
Pinterest usernames are 3 to 30 characters and can include letters, numbers and underscores. They become part of your profile URL, so shorter is easier to share.
Can I change my Pinterest username?
Yes. Open your profile, choose Edit profile and update the username field. If the name is free, Pinterest saves it. Because your profile URL changes, update any links you've shared.
Does my Pinterest username help with search?
A little. Pinterest uses profile text and board names heavily, so keywords in your display name and boards do more. A relevant username still helps people understand what you pin about.
What's the difference between a Pinterest name and a username?
Your name is the display title on your profile and can be longer with spaces. Your username is the unique handle used in your URL and mentions.
Will I lose my pins or followers if I change my username?
No. Your pins, boards and followers stay on your account. Only the handle and link change, so update your website, bio and other social links.
