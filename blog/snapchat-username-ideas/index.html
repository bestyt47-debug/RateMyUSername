Snapchat is built around your closest circle, so your username has one main job: being easy for friends to type into the search bar. Nobody scrolls through a discovery feed to find you. They get your name from a friend, a bio link or a story and add you on the spot. If it's easy to spell and hard to mix up with someone else, you get added. If it's a jumble of numbers, you don't.

Snapchat's rules are tighter than most apps. Usernames must be 3 to 15 characters, start with a letter and end with a letter or a number. Periods, hyphens and underscores are allowed in between. That leaves less room for creativity, so we've built 100 ideas that all fit. Pick a vibe, tweak it and check it in Settings before someone else takes it.

How to pick a Snapchat username that works

A few small rules go a long way on an app that's all about quick adds.

  • Start with a letter, end with a letter or number. Snapchat rejects names that begin with a symbol or end with a period, hyphen or underscore.
  • Stay under 15 characters. That's the hard cap, so shorter names are easier to say, type and search.
  • Don't share personal details. Skip your full name, school, age or location. A username is public and hard to change.
  • Use the display name for style. Your display name can include emojis and spaces and can be changed any time, so keep the username simple.
  • Think of the long run. Snapchat limits how often you can change your username, so choose one you'll still like next year.

FAQ

How long can a Snapchat username be?
Usernames must be 3 to 15 characters. They have to start with a letter and end with a letter or number, and can include letters, numbers, hyphens, underscores and periods in between. No spaces or emojis.
Can I change my Snapchat username?
Yes, but not often. At the time of writing, Snapchat allows one username change every 12 months, so choose carefully. You can do it in Settings under Username.
What's the difference between a username and a display name?
Your username is your unique ID that friends use to add you. Your display name is what appears in chats and can include emojis and spaces. You can change your display name any time.
Will I lose my streaks or friends if I change my username?
No. Your friends, streaks and Snap Score stay with your account. Only the name people use to search for you changes, so let close friends know.
Why is the username I want unavailable?
Snapchat's namespace is crowded and old accounts hold many short names. Try adding a period, a hobby word or your initials instead of a random number string.

Great Snapchat names are easy to say across a lunch table. Test yours by saying it out loud to a friend, then rate it to see how memorable it really is.
