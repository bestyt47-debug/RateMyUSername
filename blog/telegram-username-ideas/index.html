Telegram does something few messaging apps do well: it lets you set a public username so people can message you, add you to groups or find your channel without ever seeing your phone number. That makes your handle both a privacy tool and a link. It sits in a t.me address you can paste into a bio, a QR code or a poster, and it works everywhere.

For that reason, a Telegram username needs to be short, easy to say and easy to type from memory. The rules are simple: 5 to 32 characters using letters, numbers and underscores, and it has to start with a letter. Below are 100 ideas across five styles, from cozy personal handles to community channel names. Pick one, adjust it and check it in Settings before someone else does.

How to pick a Telegram username

Since it doubles as a link, a Telegram username should be easy to share aloud.

  • Start with a letter. Telegram usernames can't begin with a number or an underscore.
  • Aim for 5 to 32 characters. Five is the minimum, and shorter is easier to type into a chat.
  • Use underscores sparingly. They're the only separator available, so one or two keeps the handle readable.
  • Separate username and display name. Your display name can hold spaces and emojis, so the username can stay clean.
  • Think about who will find you. If it's for a channel or community, include the topic so new members know what they're joining.

FAQ

How long can a Telegram username be?
Telegram usernames are 5 to 32 characters and can include letters, numbers and underscores. They must start with a letter, and each one is unique across Telegram.
Can I change my Telegram username?
Yes, any time. Go to Settings, tap your username and edit it. There's no strict cooldown, though the new name has to be free and old links stop working once you switch.
Do I need a username to use Telegram?
No, it's optional. Without one, people can only reach you through your phone number or a shared link. A username lets others find you without revealing your number.
What's the difference between a username and a display name?
Your display name is the first and last name shown in chats and can include spaces and emojis. Your username is the unique @ used in t.me links and searches.
Can someone take my old Telegram username after I change it?
Yes. Once you release a username, another user can claim it, so only change it if you're okay losing that name.
