100 Twitter (X) Username Ideas Under 15 Characters

Published Sep 20, 2026 · 6 min read · Rate My Username Team

X gives you just 15 characters to work with, and letters, numbers and underscores are all you get. Here are 100 handles that fit, sorted by vibe, plus answers to the most common questions.

On X, your handle is your whole first impression. Replies are short, timelines move fast and people decide in a split second whether to click a name they don't recognize. Every reply you post carries your handle and avatar, so a memorable one turns a good take into a follow, and a forgettable one gets scrolled past.

The catch is the 15-character ceiling. There are no periods, no hyphens and no spaces, so you have to be clever with what's left. That's actually a gift. Constraints force sharper names. Below are 100 handles that all fit within the limit, sorted into five styles from sharp-tongued to soft and cozy. Steal one, tweak it and check availability in Settings before you commit.

How to pick an X handle that fits

Fifteen characters goes fast. These habits keep your name tight.

  • Count before you commit. Handles are capped at 15 characters, so test your favorite before you fall in love with it.
  • Stick to letters, numbers and underscores. No periods or hyphens here, so an underscore is your only separator. Use it sparingly.
  • Pick a name that survives a quote tweet. When your handle gets screenshotted next to a spicy take, it should look good and read clearly.
  • Put the personality in your display name. Your display name can hold emojis, spaces and much longer text, so the handle can stay simple.
  • Think about who's reading. A witty handle suits meme accounts. A clean name-based one works better if you're networking or building a brand.

FAQ

How many characters can an X username have?
Handles can be up to 15 characters and may only include letters, numbers and underscores. Display names are longer and can include spaces and emojis.
Can I change my Twitter/X username?
Yes. Go to Settings, then Your account, then Account information, then Username. There's no strict cooldown, though the new handle must be unique and can't contain words like "admin" or "twitter."
What happens to my followers if I change my handle?
Nothing. You keep your followers, posts and likes. Old mentions of your previous handle won't link to you, and your profile URL changes, so update any links you've shared.
What's the difference between a display name and a username?
The display name is the bold text people see first and can be changed freely. The username is the unique @ used for mentions, replies and your profile link.
Why can't I get the short handle I want?
Short handles were claimed years ago. If yours is taken, try adding a verb (leo_writes), an underscore or a topic word instead of random numbers.

A good X handle is short enough to be tagged in a hurry and interesting enough that people want to check who's behind it. Narrow your list to three, then rate them before you commit.
