An Xbox gamertag is more than a username. It's your identity across your console, your PC games and your friends list, and it's attached to every achievement you unlock. When you hop into a party or a lobby, your gamertag is the only introduction you get, and a clear one makes it easier for people to add you again.

The limit is tight. New gamertags can be up to 12 characters, and if your chosen name is already in use, Xbox adds a numeric suffix to make it unique. That suffix can look messy, so a distinctive base name is worth the effort. Below are 100 ideas across five styles, all 12 characters or fewer. Pick one, tweak it and test it on Xbox.com before someone else claims it.

How to pick an Xbox gamertag that stays clean

Twelve characters is tight, so every letter needs to earn its place.

  • Aim for a distinctive base name. Common names get a numeric suffix, so an unusual word combination stays cleaner.
  • Keep it readable on a controller. Friends will have to type it with a thumbstick, so avoid tricky letter and number swaps.
  • Skip offensive or impersonating names. Xbox enforces community standards, and a flagged name can force a change.
  • Think about all your platforms. Your gamertag appears on console, PC and some cross-play titles, so choose something you'd be proud to see everywhere.
  • Remember changes aren't unlimited. Your first change is free, but later ones cost money, so choose carefully.

FAQ

How long can an Xbox gamertag be?
New gamertags can be up to 12 characters, made up of letters and numbers with optional spaces. If your name is already in use, Xbox adds a numeric suffix to keep it unique.
Can I change my Xbox gamertag?
Yes. Your first change is free, and later changes cost a fee that varies by region. You can change it from your Xbox profile settings on console or on Xbox.com.
What is the number after my gamertag?
It's a suffix Xbox adds when the name you chose is already taken. It keeps every gamertag unique. Some players pay to pick a cleaner name without it.
Will I lose my achievements if I change my gamertag?
No. Your Gamerscore, achievements, games and friends stay with your account. Only the name others see changes, so let friends know.
Is my Minecraft Bedrock name the same as my gamertag?
On Bedrock Edition, yes, it uses your Xbox gamertag. Java Edition uses a separate Minecraft profile name with its own rules.
