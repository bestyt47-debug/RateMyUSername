1. Introduction

Welcome to Rate My Username, available at https://ratemyusername.fun (the "Site", "we", "us" or "our"). We respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you visit or use the Site, how it is used, who it may be shared with, and the choices you have.

By using the Site, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Site.

2. Information We Collect

Information you enter into the tools

Usernames, bios and other text you type into our tools (for example the username rater, compare tool, roast tool, OG Bio generator, championship and GameCard) are used to generate your result on the page you are viewing. You do not need an account to use these tools.

Account information (optional)

Creating an account is optional. If you choose to sign up or log in, we collect your email address and a password so that we can authenticate you. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and are not stored by us in readable form.

Saved results and leaderboard data (signed-in users only)

If you are signed in and choose to save a result, we store that result (such as the username, score and related result details) against your account so you can see it again in "my saves". If you are signed in and rate a username, that username and its score may also appear on the global leaderboard, which is visible to other signed-in users. Your email address is not shown on the leaderboard.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, when you visit the Site certain information is collected automatically by us and by our service providers, including your IP address, browser type and version, device type, operating system, referring page, pages viewed, and the date and time of your visit. This information is used to operate and secure the Site, understand how it is used, and serve advertising.

  • To provide, operate and maintain the Site and its tools.

4. Cookies and Web Beacons

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by a website. Web beacons (also called pixels or clear GIFs) are small graphics that help a website or advertiser recognize a browser and measure things such as whether an ad was displayed. The Site and the third parties that provide services on it may use cookies, web beacons, local storage and similar technologies for the following purposes:

  • Essential and preference storage: for example, remembering your light or dark theme choice and keeping you signed in if you have an account.
  • Analytics: understanding how visitors use the Site.
  • Advertising: serving, measuring and personalizing ads, as described in Section 5.

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers let you block or clear cookies and notify you when a cookie is set. If you disable cookies, some parts of the Site (such as staying signed in) may not work correctly, and you may still see ads, but they may be less relevant to you.

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies and web beacons to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to our Site or other websites. These technologies allow Google and its partners to recognize your browser, show ads, limit how often you see the same ad, and measure how well ads perform.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this Site and/or other sites on the internet. You can also opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info/choices (Digital Advertising Alliance) or, if you are in the European Union, www.youronlinechoices.com. To learn more about how Google uses information from sites and apps that use its services, see https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, and for Google's general privacy practices, see the Google Privacy Policy.

Opting out of personalized advertising does not remove ads from the Site; the ads you see will simply be less tailored to your interests.
